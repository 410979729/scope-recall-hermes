## How credit works

- **Issue reports** are credited when they identify a real, confirmed defect or
  gap with enough evidence to act on.
- **Pull requests** are credited in git history and, when they change behavior,
  in the release notes.
- Being listed here is acknowledgement only; it does not grant repository
  access. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to contribute.
